### F1 (medium): AC-15 "the release moved" cannot occur as the text is ordered

- File: `plugins/factory/references/a3.md`, section 6 item 1 against section 7 items 1 and 2.
- Reproduce: `python3 -I tests/factory/baseline_replay.py a3change tests/factory/fixtures/baseline/a3/Q2.json` (the requirement's own case: before-items #5, #6 in series 1.2; one run item #8 in series 1.3 carrying X for the whole item). Or `bash tests/factory/test_baseline_replay.sh`, check `ac15-Q2-release-moved` (named skip).
- Expected (AC-15): `change` reads `not comparable (release moved from 1.2 to 1.3)` and each side is shown as it is.
- Actual: section 6 item 1 puts a record "in another series" in no reading, so the after-item list is empty; section 7 item 1 ("first that holds decides") then fires: `after` reads `not captured (no after-item: ...)` and `change` reads `not captured (no after-item)`. The release move is hidden, and section 7 item 2 can never fire. The text half-notices this ("records of the run that sit in another series are noted in section 7") but its order defeats it.
- Required fix: let a run-list record carrying X for the whole item be an after-item whatever its series (series 1.3 in the example), excluded only if it is a calibration or excluded record; keep item 1's exclusion for records not in the run list; then section 7 item 2 fires as written. Also reword item 1's parenthetical: "a record in no section of `series`" also matches a during-item (baseline.md section 5 puts it in no section), which would hide the during-item from `items counted` (O6).
- Re-verify: remove the skip in `test_baseline_replay.sh` (`ac15-Q2-release-moved` becomes a plain check) and mutation-check it.

### F2 (medium): nothing says when an experiment written between items is committed relative to the Start item stamp

- Files: `plugins/factory/skills/run/SKILL.md` step 5 (5.2, 5.3) and step 8.10; `plugins/factory/references/a3.md` section 5 last paragraph.
