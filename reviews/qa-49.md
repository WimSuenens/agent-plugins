## Findings

### F1: `skills/run/SKILL.md` `## Andon`, *Procedure* step 1 (contract 49-andon-process section 2; AC-5, AC-23) | low | developer | the paused stage's row is told to name the andon in Notes only

- **Reproduction.** `grep -m1 '^1\. Stamp' plugins/factory/skills/run/SKILL.md` prints step 1: "The stage row you pause names it in `Notes`: `paused by A<n>`." Nothing tells the Lead to also list the andon's wait id in that row's `Waits` column. Kind 4 subtracts only waits "named in its `Waits` column". The `Waits` column's own definition ("ids of `## Waits` entries that held this row") would cover it, but a Lead following step 1 literally writes Notes only. `bash tests/factory/test_andon_text.sh` shows the named skip `qa49-f1-paused-row-lists-the-andon-wait`; `bash tests/factory/test_andon_kind3_kind4.sh` shows the effect in `k4-f1-*`: a 90m row with a 60m held `andon` wait is not flagged when the wait is in `Waits` (30m working) and is flagged `slow: 90 against median 40 of 3 earlier rows` when it is not.
- **Expected.** The pause of a running agent is excluded from the paused row's agent-working time (AC-5: "the same row with a wait that explains its duration is not flagged").
- **Actual.** A literal reading flags the paused stage as slow, a false kind 4 caused by the andon itself. Measure 1 is not affected (it reads the waits directly).
- **Severity.** Low: the column definition makes the right reading available, and the effect is a false andon, not a wrong measure. Fix: one clause in step 1 and in the Andon-row bullet of `hansei.md`: "and list its wait id in that row's `Waits`". The skip flips to a plain check when it lands.
