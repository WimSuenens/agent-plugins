## Escaped defect
A defect found after a QA pass (in the demo, after the merge, in a later slice) is filed as an issue whose body carries `escaped from: #<slice>`, `escaped at level: <level>` and `should have fired: <table row | none>`. It becomes a regression test at the level where it escaped (written before the fix, by the developer under QA's verdict), plus a calibration signal for the trigger that should have caught it: `bash "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/escaped-defect.sh" <issue> --table docs/agents/factory.md` prints the line for the hansei's `### Escaped defects` section and the row it counts against, or `no trigger fired: row missing` when no row fits (`hansei.md`, *Escaped defects*). The lead may write test depth `full` into the plan of the next slice in that area (`run-roles.md`).
**A calibration experiment** is the same loop for the flow table: `calibration.sh` proposes at most one row change, the lead decides at the hansei and records it, and the change is measured over the next 3 slices of its band against the band's baseline reading (`references/hansei.md`, *Calibration*; `references/a3.md`, section 11).
