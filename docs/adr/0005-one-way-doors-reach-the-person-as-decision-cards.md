# One-way doors reach the person as decision cards

No factory role decides a one-way door. The product-owner frames it as a decision card in everyday words (consequences for users, cost and time, and the effort to undo it, with a recommendation) and the lead puts it to the person, offering ways to dig deeper (`/research`, `/prototype`, `/grilling`, or an explanation by example). The person deciding is often not technical, so the card describes consequences rather than mechanisms; `docs/agents/factory.md` sets the level per repo.

## Considered options

Letting the product-owner agent decide was rejected: a one-way door is exactly the decision wayfinding keeps with a human.
